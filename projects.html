Danial

In development

AI customer service that replies on every channel a shop uses.

Live demo

Show Danial something.

Drop a photo, a voice note or a short video, the way a customer would. He tells you what he understood and writes the reply.

Waiting for a file.

What Danial understood

His reply

Your file is sent to the AI once to be read, then thrown away. Nothing is stored. A demo with no real shop behind it.

How I built itProblem · what I built · role

The problem

A small shop owner answers the same questions all day, on five different apps, and still misses messages.

What I built

  • Auto-reply on WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook.
  • Replies to Google reviews, and to comments on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X and Threads.
  • Books appointments on his own.
  • Understands photos, videos and voice notes, so he knows what the customer is showing him.
  • Three steps for the owner: teach him the shop, see how he replies, then train and deploy.

The rule

He never refunds, promises or shares customer details without the owner.

In-house tools

In use · internal

The team’s own bench for testing AI image and video models, and the back office behind the image engine.

How I built itProblem · what I built · role

The problem

Six AI suppliers, six websites, six bills. Nobody could compare results or see what was being spent.

What I built

  • One page with six supplier benches: same prompt, side-by-side results.
  • Live spend per page and per supplier, so cost is seen before it grows.
  • A back office for the image engine: models, scenes and wardrobe waiting for review.
  • A coverage map that shows which kinds of people the model library is still missing.

The rule

My idea and my build. I found, set up and connected every supplier’s API myself. Internal only, behind an admin token.

04 / Agent · 2026

Autopilotclaim

Live

An AI that reads accident photos and files the insurance claim by itself.

autopilotclaim.com ↗

How I built itProblem · what I built · role

The problem

Each claim is a bundle of PDFs and up to 180 photos, typed by hand into a slow web system.

What I built

  • AI visual reading: it looks at each photo of the damaged car and names the part.
  • A custom vision model I trained for this one job.
  • A fully automated workflow: read the claim PDFs, create the case, fill the report, upload every photo, build the repair estimate.
  • It logs each case in a sheet and reports back when the batch is done.

The rule

Every correction becomes a written rule, so the same mistake never happens twice.

05 / Infra · 2026

minifarm

In use · internal

Control every device from one device.

How I built itProblem · what I built · role

The problem

Eleven render machines and one controller, all looking the same. Checking them meant walking to each one.

What I built

  • One dashboard that mirrors the live screen of every machine, with its load and heat.
  • Control any of them from a single device, inside the page. No limit on how many.
  • A Blender render farm: drop a file on a web page and the frames are split across all the Macs.
  • A full handover guide, so another person or AI agent can run it.

The rule

The owner types every password. Nothing secret is stored in the code.
